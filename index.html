How to care for your turtle

  1. Tap open water in Top view to place a bloodworm. Each worm eaten reduces Hunger by 0.1. Worms sink to mid-water or the bottom and disappear after three minutes.
  2. Swipe to play or switch the Light. Each action reduces Boredom by 0.1.
  3. Top and Side show the same 3D tank. The brown platform occupies the right side, with water beneath it. The turtle can swim, dive and climb onto land.
  4. Sleep happens automatically: three possible 15-minute daytime naps and night sleep after sunset and five minutes of darkness. Hold the tank for two seconds to request sleep/wake.
  5. Awake dives are limited to 20 minutes, sleeping dives to three hours. Surfacing and breathing recovery are automatic.
  6. Health begins at 10. Hunger and Boredom outside 1–4 each cost 0.1 Health per hour; water outside 14–28°C costs 0.2 per hour. Good daytime care can restore 0.05 per hour (maximum 0.5 per day). At the end of a complete local day, sleeping longer than daylight adds 0.1 Health; otherwise deduct 0.3. Death occurs at Health 0 or Hunger 5 for more than one continuous hour.
  7. AirCon cools 1°C per 30 minutes, Heater warms 1.5°C per 30 minutes. With both off, water moves toward its natural target by 0.2°C every five minutes.
  8. After death, hold the bottom strip for two seconds to start a new turtle.

These are fictional game rules, not actual turtle-care recommendations.
